### What to Watch For

- Hypotheses that aren't testable — "patients will like it" is not a hypothesis
- Usability test observers who jump to solutions instead of observing — enforce "just watch, don't help"
- Outcome mapping that conflates usage metrics with value metrics

### Facilitation Tips

- Run the usability test live in the room: one person tests, others observe in silence and take notes
- For hypotheses: "How would you know you were wrong?" — if they can't answer, the hypothesis is too vague
- This is a capstone session — invite teams to present their full 3-session journey at debrief

### Extension / Coaching Office Hours

Participants can continue extension work independently and bring it to **Coaching Office Hours**.
At Office Hours, focus on: what decision did they make, why, and what would they change?

### Your Starting State

You have:
- `session-2-output/user-flows.md` — approved flows from Session 2
- `templates/hypothesis-template.md` — format for outcome hypotheses

Your goal: prototype the new patient intake flow, test it with one person, and define outcome hypotheses for top 3 features.

### Step by Step

**Step 1:** Build a clickable lo-fi prototype in Miro covering the new patient intake start-to-submit flow.

**Step 2:** Run a 5-minute think-aloud usability test with one other participant. Document: confusion points, hesitations, errors.

**Step 3:** Write outcome hypotheses for the top 3 features: "We believe [feature] will [outcome] for [persona]. We'll know it worked when [metric]."

### What Good Looks Like

A prototype that reveals at least one surprising usability finding. Hypotheses with specific metrics, personas, and falsifiable success criteria.

See the `solutions/` directory for reference examples — but try the exercise first.

### Extension Work

- Design the A/B test for your top hypothesis
- Write a one-page brief summarising all 3 sessions to hand off to engineering
- Bring your prototype + the hypothesis you're most uncertain about to Coaching Office Hours

Bring your extension work to **Coaching Office Hours**. You'll get 15 minutes of focused feedback.
