# Patient Intake Portal — Stakeholder Interviews
**Clinic:** Community Health Centre (fictional — for training purposes)
**Context:** Paper intake forms, 22-minute average intake time, high admin overhead

---

**Patient 1 — Maria, 67, first visit:**
"I came in with my daughter because I don't read English well. The form was in English only. My daughter had to translate everything. Some of the medical terms even she didn't understand. We sat there for 25 minutes just filling out the form. My appointment was for 10am. By the time I got in to see the doctor it was 10:40."

---

**Patient 2 — James, 34, returning patient:**
"I've been coming here for three years. Every time, I fill out the same form. My address hasn't changed. My insurance hasn't changed. My medications haven't changed. Why am I writing this out again? It feels like they don't have any record of me."

---

**Intake Nurse — Diana, 8 years at clinic:**
"My job is supposed to be clinical. Half my day is data entry. A patient hands me the form, I type it into the system. Sometimes I can't read the handwriting and I have to go back and ask them to clarify. Sometimes they've already gone home by the time I realise there's an error. The address they wrote doesn't match what's in the system from last year and I don't know which is right."

---

**Medical Records — Kevin, 2 years at clinic:**
"We get subpoenas for records periodically. When that happens, I have to go find the paper forms from the date of service. Sometimes they're misfiled. Sometimes they've been damaged. We had a water leak two years ago and lost three months of intake forms. HIPAA auditors are not pleased when you can't produce records."

---

**Admin Director — Sarah:**
"Our intake process takes 22 minutes on average. The industry benchmark for a digital intake is 8 minutes. That's 14 minutes of wasted waiting room time per patient. We see 80 patients a day. Do the math."

---

**Patient 3 — Carlos, 28, anxious about first visit:**
"I looked up reviews of this place before I came. People said the wait was long and the paperwork was confusing. I almost went somewhere else. When I got there, the form asked for my 'primary care physician's NPI number.' I had no idea what that was. I left it blank. The nurse seemed frustrated."
