# Current Paper Intake Form — Structure
**Pages:** 3 (front and back each)
**Languages:** English only
**Completion time:** Average 22 minutes for new patients, 15 minutes for returning

---

## Page 1: Patient Demographics
- Full legal name
- Date of birth
- Gender (M/F only)
- Social Security Number (last 4)
- Home address (street, city, state, zip)
- Mailing address if different
- Phone (home, cell, work)
- Email address
- Emergency contact (name, relationship, phone)
- Primary care physician name + NPI number
- Referring physician name + NPI number (if applicable)

## Page 2: Insurance Information
- Insurance company name
- Policy number
- Group number
- Policy holder name (if different from patient)
- Policy holder DOB
- Employer name
- Secondary insurance (same fields, repeated)

## Page 3: Medical History
- Current medications (name, dose, frequency) — blank lines ×10
- Allergies and reactions — blank lines ×5
- Previous surgeries (year, procedure) — blank lines ×5
- Chronic conditions (checkboxes): Diabetes, Hypertension, Heart disease, Asthma, Cancer (type), Other
- Family history: same checkboxes for maternal/paternal side
- Reason for today's visit (free text)
- HIPAA acknowledgement signature + date
- Financial agreement signature + date
